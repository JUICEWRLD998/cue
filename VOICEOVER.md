# Voiceover script

Copy the whole block under "Main script" and paste it into your voice generator in one go.
The block has only the spoken words: no labels, no stage directions, no numbers. A blank line separates
paragraphs and marks a natural pause. The paragraphs match shots 1 to 7 in [DEMO.md](DEMO.md).

Length: about 150 words, which is 55 to 60 seconds at a calm pace. The remaining time in the 70 second cut is the
silent Drop in shot 5.

## Voice settings to ask for

- Calm, warm, confident. A clear speaking voice, not an announcer, not a salesperson.
- Pace: slightly slower than conversation. Short pauses at each blank line.
- One speaker, no music in the generated file.
- Export WAV or MP3 at 44.1 or 48 kHz.

## Main script

```text
This is a drum machine. And its song is not a file. It is a document in Sanity.

Every sound you hear is made in the browser, from numbers stored in the dataset. No samples. No audio files. Just a paper roll, with one hole for every hit.

In Sanity, a document has two versions: the published one, and the draft. Cue turns that into a club. The room plays what is published. The DJ plays the draft, alone, in headphones.

Here is the Deck. I change the pattern. A clap on the two and the four. A bass note on the one. The room has not heard a thing. Only I can, with the cue switched on.

Ready. Drop.

Behind it is one small schema: voices, songs, sections, lanes and steps. The patterns live inside the song, so publishing is one change, and the room never plays a half finished track.

Anyone can try the idea in the booth, with no login. Cue. Published is the room. Drafts are the headphones.
```

## Short script (optional, for a 20 second voiced cut)

```text
This drum machine is a Sanity document. The room plays what is published. The DJ hears the draft, alone. Press Drop, and the song changes on the next bar. Cue. Published is the room. Drafts are the headphones.
```

## Notes for the edit (not part of the paste)

- After "Ready. Drop." leave the voice track empty for about six seconds. The Drop is the moment; let the beat speak.
- If the Deck does not work on the day and you film the Studio instead, change "Here is the Deck" to "Here is the
  Studio", and "with the cue switched on" to "in my draft". Everything else stays true.
- If your generator mispronounces "Cue", write it as "Q" in the paste.
- Do not add the words "instant", "AI-powered" or "revolutionary". They are not true or not needed.
